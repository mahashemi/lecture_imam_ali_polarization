\qaframe{Recap}{%
  \qa{What is the universal law of attraction, and how did ancient people's understanding differ from it?}
     {Modern science holds that every atom in existence, from the smallest particle to the largest mass, is under a general law of attraction. Ancient people only recognised \emph{particular} instances of it (e.g.\ magnet and iron, amber and straw) -- they never grasped the universal principle behind them.}
  \qa{What are the four types of people, and which type does 'Ali belong to?}
     {(1) Attract nothing, repel nothing -- like a slab of rock; (2) attract everyone, repel no one -- usually hypocrisy, since real conviction always creates friction; (3) repel everyone, attract no one; (4) attract \emph{and} repel -- people of real conviction. 'Ali is Type 4, and his was the strongest example in history.}
  \qa{Why is having strong attraction and repulsion not, by itself, proof of good character?}
     {Strength of both powers only shows conviction, not virtue -- what matters is \emph{who} is drawn in and \emph{who} is driven away. Even tyrants such as Genghis Khan, Hajjaj, and Mu'awiyah had both powers strongly.}
